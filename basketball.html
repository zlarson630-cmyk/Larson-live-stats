    Quick guide & keyboard commands

    Choose H for home or V for visitor. J = 2-point shot, Y = three, E = free throw, F = personal foul, C = technical foul, O = timeout, then H or V for the team. For a technical in player-tracking modes, enter a jersey or press N for team / bench. Type the jersey number, then G for made or M for missed. The controls advance as you type. Press Backspace to go back one step in the current play. Always enter two digits for jerseys, such as 05 for #5. Basic records makes immediately without a player. Streamlined records makes after the player. In Full Suite, press G for a make, O for a miss with an offensive rebound, or D for a miss with a defensive rebound.

    Basic example: H J (two team points). Streamlined: H Y 11 (three points to #11). Full Suite: H Y 11 G 02 (assist by #2) or H J 11 D 04 (miss and visitor #4 defensive rebound). N skips an assist, rebound or steal prompt. After D or O, enter the rebounder’s jersey, press M for a team rebound, or press Enter to assign the jersey later. M at the shot result still records a miss and follows your rebound prompt setting. Use Assign rebounder in Game Flow when you have the number. Space or F8 starts or stops the clock, including while typing a play. In Full Suite, T records a turnover and switches possession once the turnover is saved. Press S at the steal prompt to credit the opponent or N for no steal. R = rebound and K = block. A foul asks for H or V. Basic saves a team foul immediately; Streamlined and Full Suite also ask for the player: F V 24. Phone buttons keep +1, +2 and +3 visible for both teams, then ask who scored.

    Use the touch buttons for the same steps. The active team's jersey color follows ball possession. Select H or V, or use the team buttons, whenever possession changes. Saving an assist or pressing Enter after a make switches the scoring team to the opponent. At the assist prompt, Enter records no assist and switches possession. Use H or V to switch teams manually. Use Start game · opening tip to choose the winner and start the clock. For a jump ball during play, press B, then H or V for the team awarded the ball. The arrow moves to the other team. You can also adjust the arrow in Game controls.

    To correct a play, use Game Flow. Undo removes the latest play and its linked rebound, block or steal. Download One-page PDF during a timeout, or Full stats PDF for the detailed report.
